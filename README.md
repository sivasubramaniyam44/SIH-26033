## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

FarmConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), consumers and bulk buyers through a common platform.

Farmers can register and upload their agricultural products, available quantities, prices and expected harvest dates. Consumers and bulk buyers can browse products, compare prices and purchase directly from farmers without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform includes an AI-based demand forecasting system that analyses previous orders, seasonal trends and market requirements to predict future demand. It also provides group ordering facilities, where orders from nearby consumers are combined to reduce transportation costs.

Additionally, the platform provides logistics support through delivery partner integration and intelligent route optimization.

2. How it addresses the problem

Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

Eliminates unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

Provides transparent pricing and direct order management.

Combines nearby consumer orders to reduce transportation expenses.

Uses AI to forecast demand and minimize unsold agricultural produce.

Optimizes delivery routes to reduce fuel consumption and delivery delays.

Provides farmers with access to larger markets.

3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

The uniqueness of FarmConnect lies in integrating three intelligent features into a single agricultural marketplace:

Smart Demand Aggregation: Combines orders from nearby consumers into bulk orders, reducing delivery costs.

AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Predicts future demand using historical sales, seasonal trends and purchasing patterns, helping farmers plan their production.

Intelligent Route Optimization: Identifies efficient delivery routes based on buyer locations, order quantities and vehicle capacity.

An additional feature is a Harvest Availability Calendar, which allows farmers to display expected harvest dates so buyers can plan their purchases in advance.

## Technical Approach
1. Technologies to be used

Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Mobile Application: Flutter.

Backend: Python with Django.

Database: PostgreSQL / Firebase.

Artificial Intelligence: Python and Scikit-learn.

Demand Forecasting: Machine Learning and Time-Series Forecasting.

Route Optimization: Google Maps API and optimization algorithms.

Location Services: GPS.

Payment Integration: UPI Payment Gateway.

2. Methodology and process for implementation

Step 1 – User Registration: Farmers, FPOs, consumers and bulk buyers register on the platform.

Step 2 – Product Listing: Farmers upload agricultural product details, available quantities, prices and expected harvest dates.

Step 3 – Demand Collection: Consumers and bulk buyers browse products and submit their requirements.

Step 4 – AI Demand Forecasting: The system analyses historical orders, seasonal demand and purchasing patterns to predict future demand.

Step 5 – Smart Order Matching: The platform matches buyers with farmers based on product availability, quantity, price and geographical location.

Step 6 – Smart Order Aggregation: Orders from nearby consumers are combined to reduce transportation costs.

Step 7 – Logistics Optimization: The system identifies efficient delivery routes using location data and route optimization algorithms.

Step 8 – Order Completion: Farmers receive confirmed orders, consumers make payments and products are delivered through farmers or delivery partners.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea

The platform can be developed using existing web and mobile application technologies.

Open-source machine learning libraries can be used for demand forecasting.

GPS and mapping services can support location-based matching and route optimization.

The application can initially be implemented in a single village or district and gradually expanded.

FPOs can help onboard multiple farmers and aggregate agricultural produce.

The system can be developed in phases, starting with direct ordering and gradually integrating AI features.

2. Potential challenges and risks

Limited smartphone usage and digital literacy among some farmers.

Inaccurate demand predictions due to changing weather and market conditions.

Transportation difficulties in rural and remote locations.

Product quality and quantity mismatches.

Delays in payments and order fulfilment.

Limited historical data for training accurate machine learning models.

3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges

Provide a simple multilingual interface and assisted registration through FPOs.

Continuously improve forecasting models using updated sales data.

Partner with local delivery services and transportation providers.

Introduce product verification, ratings and quality checks.

Use secure payment gateways and order confirmation systems.

Initially implement the system on a small scale and improve it using real-world operational data.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential impact on the target audience

FarmConnect primarily benefits small and marginal farmers, Farmer Producer Organisations, individual consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk agricultural buyers.

The platform creates a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain by providing direct market access, better price visibility and organized logistics support.

2. Benefits of the solution

Economic Benefits:
